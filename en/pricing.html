Questions

Does the subscription renew by itself?

No. A monthly code lasts 31 days. When it runs out, Vio tells you: pay again from the button and you get a new code. No surprise charges.

My code didn't arrive

Wait 15 minutes and check your spam folder too. If it isn't there, maybe the payment note didn't contain your email: reply to PayPal's confirmation email or write to us, and we'll send it by hand.

Can I use it on more than one computer?

Yes, on your own computers. The code is personal: don't pass it on to others.

What happens if I don't buy?

After the 14-day trial Vio stops taking requests until you enter a code. Your projects and files stay yours, on your PC.
